NextGen Corp: HR Workforce Analytics

Conducted an end-to-end HR analytics project for a multi-department organisation, querying and transforming employee data using SQL to uncover critical workforce trends. Identified that the Engineering department carried the highest turnover rate (46.43%), personal and career-growth reasons were the dominant exit drivers, and salary compression existed across all job titles — with average pay clustering near $81K regardless of role or seniority. Performance ratings averaged 4.1 across the board, raising concerns around evaluation inflation. Delivered a fully interactive Power BI executive dashboard surfacing turnover patterns, tenure insights, salary distribution, and department-level performance metrics, with targeted recommendations to improve retention and introduce differentiated compensation structures.

NovaMed Solutions: Pharmaceutical Sales & Revenue Analysis

Designed a multi-page Power BI dashboard analysing 2023 sales data for a pharmaceutical distributor generating $71M in revenue at an 82% profit margin across 200 customers in 6 countries. Identified Doxycycline and Lisinopril as top-revenue drugs, pinpointed Montelukast as a high-volume but critically low-margin product ($32K profit on 7,500+ units), and revealed that Canada dominates revenue while the US underperforms relative to market size. Seller-type customers drove 89% of total revenue, confirming a B2B-heavy model. Delivered actionable recommendations to rationalise the product portfolio, expand preferred-customer programmes into European markets, and reduce distributor dependency.

Choco De Lux: European Operations & Workforce Analytics

Built a dual Tableau dashboard analysing the regional operations and workforce performance of a premium artisanal chocolate brand operating across Europe. Flagged a critical margin risk: COGS (£2.77M) exceeded net profit (£2.36M), with a 4.4% cancellation rate compounding profit erosion. Delivery success ranged from 100% in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes to just 92.7% in Brussels and Hamburg. Workforce analysis revealed only 6 of 20+ salespeople met the £100K profit target, with Karlen McCaffrey identified as a top-performer outlier. The website channel drove 45.5% of total sales impact, far outpacing social media. Recommendations spanned supplier renegotiation, logistics best-practice replication, and a tiered commission structure to lift team-wide performance.

Café Harmony: Sales Performance & Customer Experience Analysis

Developed a comprehensive Excel dashboard for a multi-location café chain, analysing sales trends, product performance, stock levels, location productivity, and customer sentiment across 1,003 customers and 4 city locations. Muffins and Cappuccinos emerged as the top revenue drivers, while Suburban locations significantly outperformed Downtown, Airport, and Uptown outlets. Negative customer sentiment (1,048) outpaced positive feedback (960), and a sharp Q4 sales decline from £1,200+ in September to a year-low in November highlighted a structural demand gap. Delivered recommendations on seasonal promotions, product bundling, location-specific strategy, and service quality improvements to reverse the late-year revenue drop and lift customer ratings from average to exceptional.
